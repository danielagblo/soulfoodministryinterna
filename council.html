Ministerial Council

Unite, train, equip, ordain, and support.

The Soul Food Ministerial Council is a ministerial leadership and fellowship body established under the vision and leadership of Bishop Dr. Eddie Letsa, who serves as its President. The Council brings ministers of the Gospel together for spiritual growth, ministerial development, unity, accountability, and effective service in the Kingdom of God.

What the Council provides

A platform for the training, recognition, ordination, mentoring, and advancement of qualified men and women of God.

Mission and vision

The Council believes that ministry is a divine responsibility requiring spiritual maturity, godly character, knowledge, discipline, and commitment. Its goal is to raise, equip, and unite servants of God who will faithfully advance the Gospel of Jesus Christ and influence the Church and society.

  • Mission: to unite, train, equip, ordain, and support ministers of the Gospel for effective and responsible Kingdom service.
  • Vision: to raise a united, disciplined, and spiritually empowered body of ministers who will demonstrate integrity, excellence, and faithfulness in advancing the Kingdom of God.
  • The Council upholds biblical principles, promotes sound ministerial ethics, and creates opportunities for greater impact in ministry.

Biblical and practical training for ministry is also offered by the Soul Food Ministerial College, including the School of the Prophets – Prophets Academy. Bishop Dr. Eddie Letsa directs that college and presides over this Council.
