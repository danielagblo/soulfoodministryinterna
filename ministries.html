The founder

Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta is a computer specialist who carried that work into the church. The Ghanaian Times has described him as the founder and General Overseer of Soul Food Ministries International. News reports often spell his surname Letsa.

In April 2014 he told the Ghana News Agency that the ministry had launched an internet television station, soulfoodtv.tv, so the word, testimony, prayer, and the life of the church and its computer college could be heard beyond the building. He gave the station the motto the ministry still uses. Later that year he said the church’s free computer training began because he had a call from God to bless people and improve society.

“As we seek the kingdom of God, it is also important that we empower people physically whilst we talk about the spiritual things.” Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta, Ghana News Agency, December 2022

Two schools of the same house

The church trains people for ministry and, separately, teaches computer work at no charge. Enrolment for either school is arranged with the ministry.

Ministerial College

The college trains and equips people who have a call to serve, ordains them, and gives a licence to minister. Its teaching is set on Matthew 28:19, “Go and make disciples,” and Mark 16:15, “Go and preach to all nations.”

It runs three schools side by side. Impartation is training to work with the Holy Spirit for healing and impartation, at any stage of faith. Prophets is what it means to prophesy, and how that work is done. Ordination is learning to stand before a congregation and preach.

The syllabus it describes covers biblical foundations, help in discerning a calling, practical ministry, preparation for mission and church planting, and the work of a pastor: counsel, visiting the sick, and assisting the poor. The stated aim is to raise leaders who can plant churches and serve people of different cultures, means, and ages, in Ghana and beyond. The college has not published a list of named degrees or an outside accreditation.

ICT College

Soul Food ICT College is free. The church pays, and the school is open to anyone who wants to learn, not only members. Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta, a computer specialist, is the director. The programme began in 2009 under the name Free for All.

By December 2022 he told the Ghana News Agency that more than 17,000 people had been trained over 13 years, and that most were in work. A Sunday class has been advertised from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., once a week. That class is the college, not the Revelation Service on the same morning. Text your name and telephone number to 024 488 9424 and ask which cohort is open.

The published courses include network engineering, digital security, CCNA, network information security, mobile phone technology, phone systems and Voice over IP, and business management with internet entrepreneurship.
