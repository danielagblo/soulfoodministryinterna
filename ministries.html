The church

Soul Food Ministries

Soul Food Ministries International is the church in Lapaz, Accra, led by Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta as General Overseer. Its published motto is “Changing the World by the Word.”

The founder

Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta is a computer specialist who carried that work into the church. The Ghanaian Times has described him as the founder and General Overseer of Soul Food Ministries International. News reports often spell his surname Letsa.

In April 2014 he told the Ghana News Agency that the ministry had launched an internet television station, soulfoodtv.tv, so the word, testimony, prayer, and the life of the church and its computer college could be heard beyond the building. He gave the station the motto the ministry still uses: “Changing the World by the Word.” Later that year he said the church’s free computer training began because he had a call from God to bless people and improve society.

“As we seek the kingdom of God, it is also important that we empower people physically whilst we talk about the spiritual things.” Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta, Ghana News Agency, December 2022

Two schools of the same house

That is why the church carries two colleges. Soul Food Ministerial College trains people who have a call to serve. Soul Food ICT College teaches computer skills at no charge, paid for by the ministry and open to anyone willing to learn.

Gatherings

These are the services the ministry has published:

  • Revelation Service, Sunday, 8:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.
  • Evening Service, Monday to Saturday, 5:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
  • One-to-one counselling, Monday, Thursday, and Saturday, 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.

The church’s music crew is part of the worship. Ask the ministry to confirm the current week before you travel.
