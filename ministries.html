Mission and vision

Our mission

To win souls, nurture believers, equip ministers, and raise effective Kingdom leaders.

Our vision

To become a transforming instrument of God’s Kingdom, raising people of faith, character, knowledge, and spiritual influence.

The ministry is dedicated to prayer, worship, evangelism, discipleship, leadership development, and community transformation. Through outreach, ministerial training, and its educational institutions, it seeks to equip individuals, empower leaders, and impact lives with the Word of God.

Bishop Dr. Eddie Letsa

Bishop Dr. Eddie Letsa is a visionary servant of God, a respected minister of the Gospel, spiritual leader, educator, and entrepreneur, committed to advancing the Kingdom of God through ministry, leadership development, and education.

He is the Founder and Senior Bishop of Soul Food Ministries International, where he provides spiritual leadership and direction. He also serves as the Director of Soul Food ICT College, the Director of Soul Food Ministerial College – Prophets Academy, and the President of the Soul Food Ministerial Council, an institution dedicated to training, equipping, and ordaining ministers for effective service in the Kingdom of God.

Through these roles he remains passionate about raising spiritually mature leaders, empowering individuals with knowledge and practical skills, developing ministers, and transforming lives through the Word of God.

“As we seek the kingdom of God, it is also important that we empower people physically whilst we talk about the spiritual things.” Bishop Dr. Eddie Letsa, Ghana News Agency, December 2022
