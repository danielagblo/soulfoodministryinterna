The skill

ICT College

Soul Food ICT College is the free computer school of Soul Food Ministries International. Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta, a computer specialist, is the director. The church pays for the training. It is open to anyone who wants to learn, not only members of the church.

The founder

In August 2014 he told the Ghana News Agency that Free for All began because he had a call from God to use his skill to bless people and improve society. By then the programme had been running for five years. He has said the difference between a developed country and a developing one is how they use technology, and he told that graduating class, “Your life belongs to you, and you must drive it yourself.”

“I am a computer specialist and that is what I carry into ministry. It is good that what you carry and serve God with, you make it a blessing to people.” Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta, Ghana News Agency, December 2022

The college says the programme began in 2009 under the name Free for All. By December 2022 he reported that more than 17,000 people had been trained over 13 years, and that most were in work. Graduations covered by Graphic Online have included three-month cohorts. The aim is employable skill and a way for graduates to start work of their own. The college has said it keeps in touch with graduates after they leave.

Courses the college publishes

  • Honors Diploma in Network Engineering. Hardware (A+), networking (N+), Windows Server, and Linux fundamentals.
  • Honors Diploma in Digital Security. CCTV systems, satellite-dish installation and configuration, and intrusion detection.
  • Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA). Installing, configuring, operating, and troubleshooting a medium-sized network.
  • Network Information Security.
  • Mobile phone technology.
  • Phone systems and Voice over IP, including iPBX.
  • Business management and internet entrepreneurship, taught with the technical work so graduates can start a business.
