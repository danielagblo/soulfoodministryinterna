ICT College

Knowledge is power. Skills create opportunities.

Soul Food ICT College is the technology and skills institution of Soul Food Ministries International. Bishop Dr. Eddie Letsa is the Director. The college provides computer education, practical ICT training, and digital skills for people building a career in technology.

17,000

By December 2022

By December 2022, Bishop Dr. Eddie Letsa told the Ghana News Agency that more than 17,000 people had been trained over 13 years, and that most were in work. The programme had begun in 2009 under the name Free for All.

Areas of training

The college teaches practical Information and Communication Technology, and other emerging technology fields. Its commitment is accessible ICT education, competent IT professionals, and employable skills for a digital world.

Before you enrol

Write to the college and ask which class is open before you travel.

  • Text your name and telephone number to 024 488 9424, or call 059 737 3632 or 059 737 3633.
  • Name the area you want: hardware and maintenance, networking, software applications, systems administration, or computer repairs.
  • Email eddieletsa@gmail.com.

How to register

A class meets at Lapaz. Text your name and telephone number to 024 488 9424, or call 059 737 3632 or 059 737 3633, and the college will call you back.
