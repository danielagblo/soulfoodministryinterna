ICT College

Free for all. The church pays.

Soul Food ICT College is the free computer school of Soul Food Ministries International. Bishop Dr. Eddie Lesta, a computer specialist, is the director. The church pays the training. It is open to anyone who wants to learn, not only members of the church. The programme began in 2009 under the name Free for All.

17,000

By December 2022

More than 17,000 people trained over 13 years. He said most were in work. The programme began in 2009 under the name Free for All. In August 2014 he told the Ghana News Agency it had then been running for five years, after a call from God to use his skill to bless people and improve society.

Courses the college publishes

Seven courses are named in public. Where a description has been published, it is given here. Where only the title is published, ask the college what the present class covers. The college has not published Cisco exam fees, and it has not said that it issues the Cisco certification.

Honors Diploma in Network Engineering

Hardware (A+), networking (N+), Windows Server, and Linux fundamentals.

Honors Diploma in Digital Security

CCTV systems, satellite-dish installation and configuration, and intrusion detection.

CCNA

Installing, configuring, operating, and troubleshooting a medium-sized network. That is the description the college publishes. It is a course at Lapaz, not a claim that the college awards the Cisco certificate.

Network Information Security

Published under this title. Ask the college what the current class includes.

Mobile phone technology

Published under this title. Ask the college what the current class includes.

Phone systems and Voice over IP

Including iPBX.

Business management and internet entrepreneurship

Taught with the technical work so graduates can start a business.

Before you enrol

Registration is free. The college calls you back. Confirm the cohort before you travel.

  • Text your name and telephone number to 024 488 9424, or call 024 008 1733.
  • Name the course you want from the list above.
  • A Sunday class has been advertised from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., once a week, at Container, Lapaz. Ask which cohort is open. That class is not the church’s Revelation Service, which meets the same morning.
  • Graduations covered by Graphic Online have included three-month cohorts. Ask the length of the class you are joining. The college has said it keeps in touch with graduates after they leave.
